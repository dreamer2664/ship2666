What is actually simulated?

Exact slider-crank geometry for the specified stroke and inferred rod length. Steam availability, load, speed, smoke, wake and large-ship motion are coupled approximations—not a thermodynamic or CFD simulation. Auxiliary valve gear is not a full engineering solver.
