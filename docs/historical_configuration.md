## Exclusions (armament / wartime / refit)
- The RMG Wyllie painting shows two 4.7-inch quick-firing guns specially mounted for the 4 Aug 1889 Spithead review. These were occasion-specific, NOT standard Teutonic configuration. Per the brief, do NOT add armament or wartime modifications to the base model.
- Blue ensign / special flags were occasion-specific; standard White Star house flag applies.

## Discrepancies recorded (not averaged)
- Length: Titanic Belfast lists 565.5 ft; brief cites ~582 ft overall. These are different conventions (LBP vs overall). Both recorded; neither overwritten.
